  Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis – Power BI Project

 1. Project Overview

The Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis project is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The main purpose of this project is to analyze sales, profit, customer segments, payment modes, product categories, and monthly performance. The interactive dashboard helps businesses understand their sales trends, identify profitable products, and make data-driven decisions.

 2. Project Objectives

- To analyze total sales and profit performance.
- To compare sales across different customer segments and regions.
- To identify the most and least profitable product sub-categories.
- To analyze monthly profit trends for different years.
- To understand customer payment preferences.
- To visualize sales and profit data through interactive charts and maps.
- To support better business decisions using data insights.

3. Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI: Dashboard creation and data visualization.
- Microsoft Excel: Data storage, preparation, and cleaning.
- Power Query: Data transformation and cleaning.
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Creating measures and calculating business KPIs.
- Data Visualization: Charts, graphs, maps, and KPI cards.

 4. Dashboard Features

- Regional Filter: Filter data by Central, East, South, and West regions.
- Sales by Segment: Donut chart displaying sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- KPI Cards: Display profit margin, average quantity, customer count, and total sales.
- Country-wise Profit: Map visualizing profit across different locations in the United States.
- Sub-Category-wise Total Profit: Horizontal bar chart comparing profitability across product sub-categories.
- Sales by Payment Mode: Donut chart showing COD, online, and card payment distribution.
- Monthly Profit by Year: Line chart comparing monthly profit for 2019 and 2020.
- Monthly Mobile Sales by Year: Line chart comparing monthly mobile sales across years.
- Quantity and Returns Analysis: Bar chart showing the distribution of return counts by quantity.

5. Skills Demonstrated

- Data cleaning and data preprocessing.
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Data visualization and dashboard design.
- Microsoft Excel and Power Query.
- Power BI report development.
- DAX calculations and KPI creation.
- Trend analysis and comparative analysis.
- Business intelligence and data-driven decision-making.

6. Project Outcomes

- Developed an interactive dashboard to monitor sales and profit performance.
- Identified differences in sales across customer segments and payment modes.
- Highlighted profitable and loss-making product sub-categories.
- Compared monthly profit and mobile sales trends between 2019 and 2020.
- Presented geographical profit distribution using a map.
- Converted raw sales data into meaningful business insights.
- Improved the ability to communicate analytical findings through visual reports.
